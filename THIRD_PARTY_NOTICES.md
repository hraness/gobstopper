## CliffCompaction

The request-time compaction engine in `crates/gobstopper-adapters/src/request/`
is a Rust port of the compaction rule, prefix store, escalation steps, and
image token estimate in CliffCompaction
(<https://github.com/nguyenvuthientrang/cliffcompaction>), described in
Trang Nguyen, Eulrang Cho, Bingqing Chen, and Tim Dettmers,
[CliffCompaction: Cost-Efficient Compaction for Long-Horizon Coding Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26779),
arXiv:2609.26779, 2026. CliffCompaction is distributed under the following
